# Refund Policy

Effective 2026-09-07. This policy applies to paid VYBZ plans, which are sold by Paddle as merchant of record. It is part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## First subscription payment

If the Service does not work as described within the first 14 days after your first paid subscription payment, email billing@vybz.cloud from the address on the organization and we will refund that payment in full. Paddle issues the refund to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank.

## Renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically each month. Renewal payments are not refunded, because the plan's included quantities are available for the whole period. To avoid a renewal, cancel before the period ends from Console → Billing → Manage subscription, or by emailing billing@vybz.cloud. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period and you keep access until then.

## Metered usage

Charges for issuances, detections, and storage beyond a plan's included quantities reflect usage already consumed and are not refundable. If you believe a usage charge is wrong, email billing@vybz.cloud with the organization name and month; we will check the audit log and correct any error.

## Duplicate or mistaken charges

Duplicate payments, and payments taken after a cancellation took effect, are refunded in full once confirmed.

## How to request a refund

Email billing@vybz.cloud with the organization name, the invoice or receipt number from Paddle, and the reason. We reply within two business days. Refunds are processed by Paddle, and the receipt for the refund comes from Paddle.

## Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the consumer protection laws of your country, where they apply to your purchase.

Contact: billing@vybz.cloud
